Physics
SS frame + SS mesh → same α → no differential thermal expansion during baking. The wave mechanism is glue softening: the adhesive loses stiffness at temperature, partially releasing the pre-tension near the border.
The wall BC spring stiffness kglue represents the in-plane stiffness of the softened adhesive per wall node. Rigid (k→∞) reproduces the current fixed-wall model. Free (k=0) models fully released glue.
