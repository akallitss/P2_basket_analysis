1. Executive summary

  • Absolute efficiency 96–97 % per station at the working point (mesh 450 V / drift 700 V), measured against 1.15 M independent uRWELL tracks per station, with a ~0.3 % systematic. The residual inefficiency is ~83 % "no response at all" (a uniform gain/threshold floor), not reconstruction losses.
  • The efficiency breathes with beam exposure. New tonight: binning inside sub-runs shows P2_MID/P2_OUT decay by 1–2.5 points with a ~30–40 min time constant after every beam-on and reset during pauses — charging-up dynamics, not a rate artefact. P2_IN is flat. Quote the settled values (≈96 % MID, ≈95 % OUT) or state the band.
  • Time resolution 15.5 (MID) / 18.4 (OUT) / 22.4 (IN) ns per station at the working point after clock-phase + walk corrections — the 20 ns P2 goal is met on two of three stations. The drift-voltage optimum (750–800 V) is confirmed; the working point sits within 0.5 ns of it.
  • Timing vs drift voltage matches Magboltz once a fitted 13.2 ns non-gas floor is added. The same model says the new Ar/CF4/iC4H10 88/10/2 gas makes the gas term negligible (3.3 ns): the next campaign will be electronics/walk-limited (~13–14 ns) rather than gas-limited.
  • The gas change is observed in data. The VMM arm took the same mesh and drift scans on both gases: the turn-on shifts by ≈ +20–25 V and the drift-latency handle shortens, both in the direction and rough size Magboltz predicts. The changeover is pinned to the run_60 overnight (P2 HV off, 1→2 Aug) — and the wrong gas labels in 136 run configs on EOS were corrected tonight.
  • The CERN-made detector story inverted: the CERN bulk chamber was the original P2_IN (23–27 Jul; repaired 26 Jul, pulled 27 Jul after visible decline), replaced on 28 Jul by a new metallic-mesh Micromegas that reaches 93 % at mesh 450 with a turn-on ~10–15 V above the CERN one.
  • One localized hardware defect found: P2_IN connector 11 reads ~0.72 efficiency in-beam (and connector 14 is dead on the fringe) — worth a cabling/FEU check before the next installation. P2_OUT is the weak station overall: ~1 point lower, per-pad distribution 4 points lower, 69 sparks (vs 15/12) and ~4 % spark dead-time in July.

2. The campaign

Five-plane setup on the H4 muon beam: two EIC uRWELL reference trackers (z = 0 / 1370 mm, DREAM FEU 1) around three P2 BASKET pad Micromegas — P2_IN (z = 320 mm), P2_MID (z = 630 mm), P2_OUT (z = 940 mm) — external scintillator/TCM trigger, DREAM readout with 16 × 60 ns waveforms. From 1 Aug the nTOF group's MX17 chamber ("Detector E") sat at z = 1155 mm.

2.1 Gas bookkeeping — fixed tonight

Every DAQ config recorded gas: "Ar/Iso 95/5" for the whole campaign. The bottle actually flowing until 1 Aug was Ar/CO2/iC4H10 93/5/2; after the overnight exchange it was Ar/CF4/iC4H10 88/10/2 (the mixture selected by the 1 Aug Magboltz study). The changeover is confirmed in data by the VMM turn-on shift and drift-latency drop (§8). Tonight the gas field of all 136 pre-changeover configs on EOS (runs, runs_meta and vmm trees, ≤ run_58) was corrected in place with an explanatory gas_note; the post-changeover runs (run_59+) are left for confirmation before being relabelled Ar/CF4/iC4H10 88/10/2.

2.2 Which chamber is the CERN one?

The EOS logbook (2026-07-28) settles it: P2_IN was the CERN bulk chamber from first beam to 27 Jul 15:00 — repaired on 26 Jul (8:1 channel), pulled after its amplitude declined through eff_nominal_1 — and replaced on 28 Jul by a new metallic-mesh Micromegas, commissioned from scratch that morning (p2in_hvrange_1/2). Both chambers therefore have full efficiency curves (§4.4), and the entire VMM campaign ran with the metallic-mesh chamber.

3. Method: absolute efficiency against uRWELL tracks

The workhorse measurement is urw_reference: two-point tracks from the front/back uRWELLs (residual spread ≤ 0.66 mm, no rotation, divergence understood as a virtual source ~40 m upstream), projected to each P2 plane through a fitted affine frame (rotation −59.7° to −60.0°, shear < 0.0013). Denominator hygiene: triggers the probe FEU never recorded (from recorded_events.npz), HV-settle and spark-veto windows are removed by time, and tracks must fall in the pad fiducial (9 mm). Efficiency = a P2 cluster within 15 mm; the probe-radius scan plateaus by 10 mm with an accidental slope of only 7×10-4 per 10 mm — read the result as ±0.3 % systematic.

Spatial resolution: 3.37–3.39 mm (x), 3.44–3.47 mm (y) rms on all three stations — exactly the 12 mm/√12 binary-readout limit. Cluster size 1.05–1.08 pads. A cross-check with deliberately mis-set mapping moves the efficiency to 60 % — "if efficiency reads far below 90 %, suspect the mapping before the detector".

*P2_IN's cold-pad loss is dominated by connector 11 (channels 704–767, in-beam efficiency 0.72 vs 0.95 for the same channels on MID/OUT) plus a nearly dead connector 14 on the beam fringe — a genuine cabling/FEU-input suspect, unique to P2_IN. 6. Stability in time

6.1 Charging-up, resolved (new tonight)

The known puzzle: highstat_eff_1 loses ~1.3 points over 2.5 h, but the recovery sub-run changed rate and pause together. The new --time-bins mode of the uRWELL stage (run on condor for all 35 sub-runs of the three efficiency runs) breaks the degeneracy:

Reading: the decay re-arms during beam-off — the signature of a charging process (with a possible smaller rate term: the second block decays slightly less at 30 % lower rate), not beam or DAQ drift. P2_IN's flatness says the effect is chamber-specific, not environmental. Recommendation for the talk: quote "96–97 % settling to ≈96/95 % after ~1 h of beam at 4.6 kHz", with this figure as backup.

8. The gas change: measurement vs Magboltz

8.1 The prediction

The 1 Aug five-mixture Magboltz study (now in gas_studies/) picked Ar/CF4/iC4H10 88/10/2: 2.4× drift velocity, flattest vd plateau, only +16 V mesh for equal gain, gas-timing floor 8.8 → 3.3 ns at the working drift field (data/gas_timing_model.csv).

8.2 The changeover, observed

What the gas comparison cannot yet say: the DREAM waveform timing only exists for gas A (the P2 pads were on the VMM, which has no waveforms and is BCID-quantised at 22.5 ns). The σ gain of the CF4 mixture is a Magboltz prediction validated in shape by the latency data — measuring it directly is the strongest argument for a short DREAM run on the new gas at the next opportunity.

11. Data-quality register and open anomalies

  • Decoder-hang holes (known, unfixed): 7 FEU files across eff_nominal_1 (sub 10/14/15), mesh_drift_scan_up_1 (drift2_11), drift_mesh_scan_1 (drift_700) sit as intact *.fdf.hang — re-decode needs the DREAM decoder binary (the DAQ machines are off). These are also the 3 failures of tonight's 35-job time-bins sweep.
  • VMM capture holes: VMM run_59 recorded no pcapng (its scan was retaken as VMM run_61); run_62 captured only 2 of 6 drift points; run_61's drift half produced no usable captures — gas-B VMM drift data = 2 points. run_25's drift sub-runs are empty.
  • Open anomaly (worth one shift-log question): the two gas-A VMM drift scans (run_26, 30 Jul vs run_57, 1 Aug — identical setpoints) differ by ~27 ns in plateau latency and by 52 vs 73 ns in low-field drift-time excess, i.e. the drift looked ~30 % slower on 1 Aug. Same direction as the old "slow bottle" mystery from the July bench studies. Temperature/pressure or flow logs would settle it.
  • eff_nominal_1 level shift: MID/OUT read 3–5 points lower on 27 Jul (drift 750) than 25 Jul (drift 700) in both methods, with the station order re-arranged; charging history and beam steering are the candidates. Recommend quoting the 25 Jul high-stat numbers (the taskplan already does) and flagging this in backup.
  • drift_mesh_2d_1: superseded by _2 (P2_MID trip); its dm_01_00–02 sub-runs are beam-off. drift_450 of drift_mesh_scan_1 = zero drift field — never fit it as a normal point.
  • Chunked sub-runs (_000/_001/_002): any analysis globbing one file per sub-run silently loses up to 57 % — all tonight's processing sums chunks.
  • Gas labels: 136 configs fixed on EOS (§2.1); run_59+ pending.
