CORE — setup

The July 2026 H4 campaign in one picture

Message: 12 days, two readout phases (DREAM telescope → VMM campaign on the same detectors), two gases, every scan type: mesh, drift, two 7×7 2D grids, high-stat efficiency, electronics config scan. 537 GB DREAM + ~400 GB VMM, all on EOS, all analysed.

  • 3 × P2 BASKET pad Micromegas between 2 EIC uRWELL trackers, SPS muons
  • Speakable numbers: 23 named runs + 51 VMM-phase runs, ~108 triggers on tape
CORE — method

Absolute efficiency against an independent tracker

Message: two uRWELL planes give 1.15 M tracks/station per sub-run; residuals are 3.4 mm rms = 12 mm/√12 — textbook binary pad response. Denominator hygiene (recorded-trigger, settle, spark-veto, fiducial) and a probe-radius systematic scan → ε to ±0.3 %.

  • Bonus credibility: a deliberately flipped ribbon in the map drops ε to 60 % — the method catches mapping errors two independent ways.

CORE — stability (new)

Charging-up, caught in the act

Message (new analysis): in 2-min bins at constant HV, MID/OUT decay 1–2.5 pp with a ~30–40 min time constant after every beam-on and reset during the pause — charging-up dynamics, chamber-specific (IN is flat). Honest headline: "96–97 % settling to ≈96/95 % after an hour at 4.6 kHz".

CORE — timing

Time resolution: the correction ladder

Message: waveform TOA with per-FEU clock-phase and walk corrections: 15.5 / 18.4 / 22.4 ns — the 20 ns P2 goal is met on two of three stations, stable to <0.5 ns over 4.5 h.

CORE — timing + gas

σ vs drift voltage — and what Magboltz says about the new gas

Message: the measured drift-scan plateau (750–800 V) is reproduced by Magboltz ⊕ a fitted 13.2 ns non-gas floor. Swap in the chosen Ar/CF4/iC4H10 88/10/2 and the gas term collapses (8.8→3.3 ns): expected ≈13.5 ns — the next campaign is electronics-limited, not gas-limited.

CORE — gas change

We already ran on the new gas — and see it

Message: the same VMM mesh scan on both gases: the turn-on shifts ≈+20–25 V — the direction and rough size of the Magboltz equal-gain prediction. The gas exchange is pinned in the HV record (P2 off overnight 1→2 Aug) and the wrong gas labels in 136 DAQ configs were corrected on EOS.

BACKUP — caveat

Putting DREAM and VMM on one axis: what had to be fixed first

SUPERSEDED 17 Aug — kept for the history. This slide used to say the two efficiencies must never share an axis, because the VMM number was a whole-station scalar over every trigger while only c4–c6 carry hybrids. That denominator mismatch is fixed: the VMM efficiency has been re-derived against the same uRWELL tracks, the same fiducial and the same probe radius as the DREAM campaign, over 78 sub-runs, so the two are now directly comparable — see the DREAM-vs-VMM slide.

Three caveats that do survive, and are carried on the comparison itself: the DREAM reference must be taken at the VMM run's own drift voltage (750 V, not highstat_eff_1's 700 V); P2_IN is a lower bound, and its DREAM reference is the 28 Jul chamber, since the original was swapped out on 27 Jul; and the cluster-size comparison is still not like-for-like — the VMM efficiency code deliberately does not cluster — so it has been dropped rather than quoted.

BACKUP — stability

The 27 Jul reference run: levels moved

Message (for questions): two days after the headline run the levels sit 3–5 pp lower and re-ordered, and the CERN P2_IN visibly declines through its final morning. Candidates: charging history, beam steering, drift 700→750. This is why the talk quotes the 25 Jul high-stat numbers.
