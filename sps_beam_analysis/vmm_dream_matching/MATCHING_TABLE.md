## Reading the table

* **in VMM window** — the fraction of DREAM events taken while the VMM was
  recording. The two DAQs are not stopped together, so this is below 100% for
  some sub_runs (run_43: the VMM stream ends a third of the way in).
* **matched (of window)** — of those, the fraction matched. This is the
  number that says whether the matching worked; the raw fraction over all
  DREAM events is in the CSV as `match_frac_dream`.
* **VMM spare** — VMM triggers with no DREAM partner: the DREAM busy veto,
  ~30% throughout.
* **rms ns** — residual spread of the matched pairs. ~10 ns everywhere it
  locks (DREAM's 5 ns hardware quantisation plus the VMM 22.5 ns BCID clock);
  ~1 µs is the random-coincidence floor, i.e. no lock.
* **trig ch** — which VMM chip:channel carried the trigger, verified per
  sub_run rather than assumed.

Per-spill detail — which spills did not match and why — is in the JSON
summaries and in the matching view of the QA page.

## The sub_runs without a lock

`run_21`, `run_24`, `run_25`, `run_26`, `run_28`, `run_29` — the whole period
before the trigger fan-out was established on 2026-07-30. `find_trigger_channel.py`
checks the busiest six channels of each and none coincides with DREAM at any
dead time from 1.5 to 100 µs. VMM 0 ch 44 and VMM 1 ch 20 are the same
fan-out line there as later — 99.6% of their hits coincide within 50 ns — but
its interval distribution is a smooth continuum (median 4.7 µs) rather than
the ≥2.4 µs dead-time structure of a real trigger stream, which is what it
becomes from run_31 on.

Four more, flagged NO-VMM, are sub_runs the VMM DAQ never recorded:
`run_25/driftscan_gap150V/200V/250V` and `run_25/meshscan_m100V` have
272-byte captures, and `run_28/cfg_gain4.5_peaktime25` wrote 135 GB of
unsuppressed noise (6.0e8 hits in two captures) with no trigger-like channel
in it.

`run_61/meshscan_m30V` is the one partial: it locks at 101 σ and matches 76%
with a 64 ns rms, against 99% elsewhere in that run. Its trigger line runs
3× the DREAM rate, so some of the matching is ambiguous. Usable, but cut on
`matched`.
