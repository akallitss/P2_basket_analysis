P2_OUT: per-pad efficiency is set by the pad's pulse height

run_46 / cfg_gain4.5_peaktime200 — VMM3a readout, SPS H4, 1 Aug 2026. Task 1 and task 3 of HANDOFF_VMM_PAD_ADC.md.

The correlation is real and it reproduces. Over 67 illuminated P2_OUT pads carrying 836,157 tracks, per-pad efficiency rises with the pad's median pulse height at Pearson r = +0.57 (p = 6e-07, Spearman ρ = +0.78) — confirming the +0.55 quoted in the handoff, which had no committed producer. It now has one.

But most of the spread is one smooth gradient across the chamber. A linear plane in (x, y) accounts for 60% of the pad-to-pad variance — 6 % of the fleet median per 10 mm — and removing it drops the pulse-height→efficiency partial correlation to +0.22. So the handoff's first open question splits in two: a chamber-scale gain gradient, which no per-channel trim can fix, and a small per-pad residual, which one could.

What was measured

Per-pad efficiency (n tracks pointing at the pad, k with a hit within probe_r) comes from eff_autopsy_report.py. Per-pad pulse height is win_adc_P2_OUT — the ADC of the nearest in-window unmasked hit — aggregated per pad. That aggregation is the missing producer; it is now committed as pad_pulse_height() in that file, storing median, 25/75 percentiles, count and a DNL-clean median per pad.

All medians quoted are DNL-corrected: the VMM3a ADC is ~2× too wide on every multiple of 16, so the median is read off a histogram rebinned to 16 codes, one full DNL period per bin. The same correction puts the P2_OUT most-probable value at 104, not the 128 in EFFICIENCY_AUTOPSY.md — 128 = 8×16 is a comb tooth. That confirms the handoff's own correction from an independent sample.

It is gain, not threshold

Direction of the correlation

A threshold artefact would run the other way: a channel with a high threshold records only its big pulses, so it would show a high median with a low efficiency. The correlation is positive, so it is real signal-size variation. Two further checks support that here — every P2_OUT chip is at the same sdt 224, and removing each chip's mean leaves the correlation intact (within-chip r = +0.66).

Cross-check: an independent, untracked measurement

The same per-pad pulse height can be had with no pcapng decoding at all, from adc_vs_ch in every capture's counts.npz — a per-channel ADC histogram that vmm_reduce.py writes whether or not --drop-columns was used. Summed over run_46's 48 captures that is 1.52×109 hits against the 837k tracked ones. The two medians agree at r = 0.975 with a spread of 9 ADC on P2_OUT, so the cheap route is usable for the rest of the campaign — the runs whose columns were dropped included.

What this does not rule out

  • It does not close the P2_OUT gap to DREAM. The best pads reach 0.962 / 0.957 / 0.943 against DREAM's 0.960, but only 2 of 67 pads are there. The mechanism is identified, not removed.
  • The residual after the gradient is not attributed. r = +0.22 survives; whether that is gas gain, mesh planarity or per-channel baseline scatter is still open, and separating them needs the per-channel trim run (qa_config.py: 'calibration': None — no such run exists).
  • Pad area is NOT the operative variable, although it correlates at r = +0.78. Across the illuminated pads the area spans only 0.16 % while the pulse height spans a factor 2.9, so the area cannot be causing it — in the fan, area is a monotone label for radius, and it is position that the pulse height follows. An earlier version of this note said “most of the spread is pad geometry”; that attribution was wrong and is corrected above. The partial-correlation arithmetic is unchanged.
  • P2_MID does not behave like this. Its pooled correlation is +0.14 (rate mask) to +0.20 (ratio mask), not the +0.52 the handoff quotes; that number did not reproduce under either mask rule. Its two chips at sdt 256 run the between-chip trend against the within-chip one. P2_MID and P2_IN are written up separately.
