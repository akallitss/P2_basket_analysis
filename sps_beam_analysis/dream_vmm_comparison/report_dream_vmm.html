P2_OUT pad-by-pad gain: VMM3a against DREAM

VMM run_46 / cfg_gain4.5_peaktime200 (1 Aug 04:53) against DREAM eff_nominal_1 / eff_nominal_00–09 (27 Jul 10:40). Same chamber, same uRWELL reference, same track selection, same operating point — mesh 450 V, drift 750 V, gap 300 V, Ar/CO2/iC4H10 93/5/2.

No — the VMM sees less pad-to-pad variation than DREAM, not more. On the 53 pads both readouts illuminate, the per-pad pulse height (each divided by its own readout's fleet median) has an rms of 0.26 on the VMM and 0.42 on DREAM, and the two agree pad for pad at r = +0.944. The VMM is a compressed copy of the same map, slope 0.58.

The variation is the detector's, and it is one smooth gradient. A linear plane in (x, y) explains 71% of it on DREAM and 68% on the VMM, in the same direction to 5°; what is left over still correlates between the two readouts at r = +0.82. Neither chain adds pad-to-pad structure of its own that the other cannot see.

What differs is what that gain map costs. The same gradient moves the VMM's per-pad efficiency over 0.50–0.96 and DREAM's over 0.92–0.99.

Why these two runs

The VMM configuration scan sat at one operating point for every cfg_* run: mesh 450 V / drift 750 V on all three P2 stations. Of the DREAM runs, highstat_eff_1 — the one the handoff quotes — is at drift 700 V, a 250 V gap, not 300. eff_nominal_1 is at 750/450, the identical point, and its 10 full-rate sub-runs give 1,770,207 fiducial tracks against the VMM's 686,411. Gas and beam are the same; the two runs are five days apart.

The drift gap is not a sensitive axis here — the VMM drift scan gives 73.9 / 74.0 / 73.6 % at gaps 300 / 350 / 400 — so highstat_eff_1 would not have been wrong, only avoidable.

The measurement, on both sides

Identical by construction: uRWELL front+back two-point tracks, the same |back−front| cut, the same free-affine frame fit onto the pad plane (both land on −60°), the same 9 mm fiducial and 15 mm probe radius, the same spark veto and recorded-event hygiene. For every pad: how many tracks pointed at it, how many it recorded, and the pulse-height distribution of the ones it did.

  • VMM — win_adc, the ADC of the in-window unmasked hit nearest the prediction. Per-pad medians are DNL-corrected (16-code bins). New producer: pad_pulse_height() in eff_autopsy_report.py.
  • DREAM — a_lead, the leading pad's amplitude, restricted to the events where the leading pad is the pad the track pointed at (87.9 % of them), which makes it the same quantity. New producer: urw_p2_padadc.py, a driver over urw_p2_efficiency's own functions.

The truncation control

The VMM's spectrum turns on at ~64 ADC against a pooled MPV of 112; the DREAM MPV is 238, so the same relative threshold is 136 DREAM ADC. A fixed cut lifts a low-gain pad's median more than a high-gain pad's, so it compresses the measured spread — the question is by how much.

At the matched cut DREAM still measures 0.38 against the VMM's 0.26; reproducing the VMM's number needs a cut near 300, which would throw away 33% of the pulses when the VMM's efficiency deficit accounts for only about 10 %. So the threshold is the mechanism but not the whole of it — a linear fit of the two per-pad medians in raw ADC gives VMM = 0.194 × DREAM +64, and that 64-ADC offset (which is the turn-on) is what pulls every ratio toward 1.

What this does not rule out

  • It does not measure absolute gain. Both axes are relative to their own readout, because the two ADCs share no scale and neither was charge calibrated in this campaign.
  • The residual compression is an additive ADC offset — resolved 2026-08-22, after this note first went up. Threshold truncation alone gets DREAM from 0.42 only to 0.38, not to 0.26; the rest is a ~43-count constant in the VMM peak ADC. Subtracting it from the VMM per-pad medians returns 0.363, the truncated DREAM number, with nothing left over — so this is no longer open, and it is not front-end compression or a non-linearity. See Why the VMM loses the weak pads, which also fits the threshold itself (162 DREAM ADC) and shows it reproduces the VMM's per-pad efficiency. A charge-injection sweep would still say what the offset is.
  • Both ADCs saturate — 1.0 % of the VMM entries in the top bin, 1.7 % of the DREAM ones. That is above the medians used here, but it is why MPVs are read with the top of the range blanked.
  • Pad area is not the driver, though it correlates at r = +0.85: across these pads the area spans 0.16 % while the pulse height spans a factor 3.9. In the fan, area is a label for radius; the pulse height follows position.
  • Five days separate the two runs. Nothing was moved and the HV is identical, but a slow gain drift between 27 Jul and 1 Aug would show up as a scale factor — which is exactly the quantity being divided out here, so it cannot affect the spread comparison. It would affect an absolute one.
  • Only P2_OUT. P2_MID and P2_IN are not in this comparison.
